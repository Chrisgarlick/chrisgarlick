# Full SEO Audit - chrisgarlick.com (2026-06-08)

Second full audit since the May 2026 site refactor. Specifically incorporates the `/for/` operating-model axis that's now live in production, but treated as a complete re-audit across both axes (`/industries/` sector + `/for/` operating model).

Previous audit: 11 May 2026 (page-level title/H1/meta issues). Most of those were tactical fixes. This audit is structural.

## Executive Summary

**Working well:**

1. Two-axis structure is conceptually sound. `/industries/<sector>` answers "what industry am I in", `/for/<model>` answers "what shape of business do I run". They're orthogonal, not competing, and an SRA-regulated solo solicitor can legitimately convert through either path.
2. Every `/for/` page ships with its own `seoTitle`, `seoDescription`, `seoKeywords`, canonical, breadcrumbs, and JSON-LD. Better hygiene than most production sites.
3. Cluster A through J already covers `/services/` and `/industries/` thoroughly.

**Critical gaps:**

1. **No Cluster K for the `/for/` axis in `topic-clusters.md`.** The pages are live but not in the cluster map. That means SEO won't push internal-linking guidance to Content or Software for `/for/` cluster pages.
2. **Zero supporting cluster pages exist for any `/for/` pillar.** All 5 pages are orphan pillars from a topical-authority perspective. No `/article/` posts feed into them yet.
3. **Cross-link gap**: 3 of 5 `/for/` pages have no `relatedIndustry` set (consultants, freelancers, tradespeople). Existing: solo-operators → law-firms, agency-starters → agencies. The other three need explicit mappings.
4. **Resource landing pages**: `/resources/<slug>` pages for the 5 lead magnets exist (per the `ForPage` component) but aren't surfaced in the keyword strategy as conversion assets.
5. **Keyword strategy** doesn't have a dedicated "Operating-Model axis" section. The `/for/` keywords live inside each Astro file but are not consolidated in `team/18-seo/keyword-strategy.md`.

**Risks if unaddressed:**

- The `/for/` pages will rank only for their own page-level keywords with no internal-link velocity from blog content. Cluster effects compound; isolated pages don't.
- The two-axis cross-link story (e.g. a sole-practice solicitor reading both `/industries/ai-for-law-firms` and `/for/solo-operators`) currently only fires from one direction.
- AI engines (Perplexity, ChatGPT) rely on internal-link density to identify topical authority. With no cluster pages, the `/for/` axis won't be cited as the canonical answer for "AI for freelancers UK" etc. - competitor sites with a single blog post and better link density will win those citations.

**Headline recommendation:**

Add Cluster K (Operating-Model Axis) to `topic-clusters.md`. Plan 4-6 supporting cluster pages per `/for/` pillar (the BOFU sits at the pillar - TOFU/MOFU posts feed it). Three of those are P1: the cost/comparison/how-to articles that map most directly to commercial intent. The rest are P2/P3 fill-in.

## Issues found (page-level)

### ISSUE 1: `/for/` pillar pages have no FAQ section

| Page | Has FAQ? | AEO impact |
|---|---|---|
| /for/agency-starters | No | Loses citation opportunity on "how do I start an AI agency UK", etc. |
| /for/consultants | No | Loses on "how do consultants use AI", "productise consulting" |
| /for/freelancers | No | Loses on "AI proposal templates", "freelance AI workflow" |
| /for/solo-operators | No | Loses on "best AI for solopreneur", "one person business AI tools" |
| /for/tradespeople | No | Loses on "AI for plumbers UK", "Google reviews automation tradespeople" |

**Fix:** Add 4-6 Q&A pairs to each `/for/` pillar via `ForPage.astro` (new prop `faqs`), wrapped in FAQPage schema. Each Q targets a People Also Ask query. Specific Qs in the per-page recommendations below.

### ISSUE 2: `/for/` pages don't link to `/article/` blog content

The pillars exist but their cluster pages don't. The `siblings` block links across `/for/` siblings (good), and `relatedIndustry` links across to `/industries/` (where present), but no `/for/` page links DOWN into supporting articles. This is the single biggest topical-authority gap.

**Fix:** Once Content writes the Cluster K cluster pages (per the recommendation below), add a "Read next" or "Deep dives" block to `ForPage.astro` listing 3-4 related articles.

### ISSUE 3: Missing `relatedIndustry` mappings on 3 of 5 `/for/` pages

| /for/ page | Current relatedIndustry | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| agency-starters | ai-for-agencies | OK |
| solo-operators | ai-for-law-firms | OK (sole-practice solicitors) |
| consultants | (none) | `ai-for-agencies` (the consultant-to-agency journey) |
| freelancers | (none) | `ai-for-agencies` (freelancers often serve agency clients) |
| tradespeople | (none) | No live industry page fits. Add prop only when a relevant `/industries/ai-for-tradespeople` is built. Until then, leave unset. |

**Fix:** Add `relatedIndustry={{ slug: 'ai-for-agencies', label: 'Established agencies' }}` to consultants.astro and freelancers.astro.

### ISSUE 4: `/for` directory landing has no JSON-LD ItemList alongside CollectionPage

Current: CollectionPage with `hasPart` array. Better: also emit ItemList schema with positions, since that improves the SERP rich-result eligibility for the directory page.

**Fix:** Add ItemList JSON-LD alongside the existing CollectionPage. Each item position = order of audiences array.

### ISSUE 5: `/for/<slug>` URLs are not referenced in `keyword-strategy.md`

The strategy doc still says "operating-model axis" only in a footnote sense. The actual `/for/` URLs and target keywords are inside each Astro file but nowhere consolidated.

**Fix:** Add a new "BOFU operating-model" subsection to `keyword-strategy.md` listing each `/for/` URL, its primary keyword, and its long-tail set. See the patch in this audit's Section 5.

### ISSUE 6: `/resources/<slug>` lead-magnet pages not in the SEO map

Each `/for/` page links to a gated resource (e.g. `/resources/one-framework-six-months-of-content`). These are linkable assets and lead-gen entry points. They're not currently:
- Listed in the keyword strategy as targets for branded long-tail terms
- Mapped in topic-clusters.md
- Verified to have their own title/meta/canonical (would need to check live)

**Fix:** Cluster J (Conversion / Trust assets) needs to add the 5 new lead magnets, each with focus keyword and a one-line content description.

### ISSUE 7: Internal link map in `topic-clusters.md` doesn't include the `/for/` axis

The ASCII diagram at the bottom of `topic-clusters.md` shows `/industries`, `/services/`, and clusters A-J. The `/for/` axis is structurally missing.

**Fix:** Update the diagram. New version proposed in the Cluster K patch.
