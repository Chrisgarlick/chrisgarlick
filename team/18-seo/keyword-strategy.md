## Location modifiers worth layering in

Use these as secondary keywords across the industry pages. Don't stuff. Sprinkle naturally in body copy + meta keywords + secondary keyword fields.

**Country:** UK, United Kingdom, GB, Britain, British
**Nations:** England, Scotland, Wales, Northern Ireland
**Major cities (for industry pages):** London, Manchester, Birmingham, Leeds, Bristol, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Cardiff, Belfast, Liverpool, Newcastle, Sheffield, Nottingham
**Regulatory:** SRA, ICAEW, ACCA, AAT, FCA, HMRC, MTD, GDPR, ICO

Do not target specific city-only keywords with their own pages yet. Layer them into existing pages' secondary keyword strings to capture city-modified searches.
