# AI & Answer Engine Optimisation (AEO)

## Goal
Get chrisgarlick.com cited by Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews when users ask about AI implementation for professional services in the UK.

## Content Structure Rules

1. **Lead with a direct answer.** First 2-3 sentences answer the target query before any preamble.
2. **Use structured subheadings.** H2s should mirror natural language questions: "How much does AI implementation cost?", "What does an AI workflow partner do?"
3. **Include a Key Takeaways block** at the top of every blog post (bulleted list after H1, before body).
4. **Use definition patterns.** "[Term] is [clear definition]." — this is what AI models cite most.
5. **Include specific numbers.** "Reduces drafting time by 60%", "£5k-8k fixed pricing". Vague claims get ignored.
6. **Use comparison tables.** AI models frequently cite tabular comparisons.
7. **Write FAQ sections.** 3-5 FAQ items per pillar page with proper heading structure.
8. **Author attribution.** Every post shows "By Chris Garlick" with link to /about.
9. **Short paragraphs.** 2-3 sentences maximum. AI models extract paragraph-level chunks.
10. **Lists over prose** for steps, features, benefits.
