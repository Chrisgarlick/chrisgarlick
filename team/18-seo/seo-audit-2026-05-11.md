### ISSUE 7: No meta keywords on any CMS page (services, home, about, contact)

The `keywords` meta tag is only present on article pages (which derive it from `focusKeyword` + `secondaryKeywords`). CMS pages (home, about, services, contact, tools, work) have no keywords tag at all.

**Fix:** The `[slug].astro` service page template passes `keywords` to SEO but the other page templates (index, about, contact) don't extract keywords from the CMS `seo` field.
