# Marketing — Final Output

## Executive Summary

The Chris Garlick brand is a personal operator brand, not a company brand. The voice is first-person, declarative, and specific — no consultant-speak, no hype, no hedging. The visual direction is dark editorial: near-black backgrounds, warm off-white text, Instrument Serif headlines, DM Mono body, warm gold (#E8D5A3) as the only accent. No gradients, no blues, no purples, no stock photography.

The messaging framework is built around three audiences (Sarah/law, Tom/agency, David/accountancy) with persona-specific messaging and objection handlers. The primary CTA is "Apply to work together" — positioning Chris as selective.

The launch plan runs from May 5–June 13, 2026: 2 weeks pre-launch (LinkedIn building-in-public + Kritano audit prep), launch day (deploy, publish, outreach), and 4 weeks of post-launch outreach and content. Target: 30 businesses audited and contacted, 5 applications received, 1 paid engagement closed within 90 days.

## Key Decisions & Recommendations

- Brand name is personal: "Chris Garlick", never a company name
- Title: "Software Developer" — never "AI expert", "AI specialist", consultant, or agency
- Voice: first person ("I"), short sentences, specifics over generalisations
- Banned words: synergy, leverage, unlock, empower, transform, journey, ecosystem, disruption, game-changer, next-level, cutting-edge
- CTA: "Apply to work together" — never "Book a call" or "Get started"
- Visual: dark mode only at V1, gold accent used sparingly, no rounded corners >4px
- Typography: Instrument Serif (headlines) + DM Mono (body) — no sans-serif
- Naming: Kritano (audit platform), Kritano CMS (content system), never "Scalar"
- Offers: The Build (£5k-8k), The Retainer (£3k-6k/mo), The Audit (free for outreach)

## Dependencies & Handoffs

**For Design:** Use the colour palette, typography, and visual rules as the foundation for the design system. The brand is austere, sharp, typographic — not friendly, not playful.

**For Content:** Follow voice and tone guidelines strictly. Reference persona-specific messaging when writing blog posts and case studies. Every piece of copy must pass the "would Chris actually say this?" test.

**For Software:** Implement the colour tokens as CSS variables. Load Instrument Serif and DM Mono from Google Fonts. "Built on Kritano CMS" in the footer. CG monogram in accent gold, top-left nav.
