# Messaging Framework

## Primary Value Proposition

I audit your operations, build software that replaces manual work, and maintain it monthly — using tools I built myself, at a fixed price, as a solo developer. Some of those systems use AI. All of them ship.

## Supporting Proof Points

1. **Proprietary tooling** — Kritano runs the audit. Kritano CMS runs the site. These aren't off-the-shelf tools rebranded. Chris built them.
2. **Fixed pricing, scoped delivery** — The Build is £5k-8k. The Retainer is £3k-6k/month. No day rates. No discovery phases that balloon.
3. **Architecture shown, not hidden** — Every case study follows Problem → Architecture → Outcome. You see what was built and how it connects.
4. **Solo developer, purpose-built tooling** — No juniors learning on your project. No layers of account management. One developer with proprietary infrastructure and AI where it fits.
5. **Sector-specific understanding** — Legal, accountancy, and agency operations are the focus. Specific workflows: document drafting, client intake, compliance prep, reporting automation.

## Top 3 Objection Handlers

### "Can you really deliver at this level as a solo operator?"
Purpose-built tooling is the answer. One developer with the right infrastructure delivers faster, cheaper, and with more consistency than a traditional team. I use AI where it makes sense, I write code where it doesn't. The site you're looking at runs on a CMS I built. That's not solo act output — it's engineering output.

### "How do I know this is secure? We handle sensitive data."
Every system is scoped to specific data flows. I don't build systems that hoover up all your data. Automations are targeted: specific documents, specific workflows, specific outputs. Data handling is documented in the architecture.

### "We've been burned before by tech projects that over-promised."
That's why the pricing is fixed and the scope is written down. The audit phase shows you what's realistic before you spend anything. I don't sell possibilities — I scope deliverables.
