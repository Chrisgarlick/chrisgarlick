# Launch Plan

## Timeline Overview

| Phase | Dates | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-launch | May 5–16, 2026 | 2 weeks |
| Launch day | May 19, 2026 (Monday) | 1 day |
| Post-launch Weeks 1–4 | May 19 – Jun 13 | 4 weeks |

## Pre-Launch (May 5–16)

### Week 1: Build in public, run audits

**LinkedIn (3 posts):**
1. "Building in public" announcement — screenshot of Kritano CMS admin. No link. Just signal.
2. Kritano audit findings on 12 law firm websites. Pure insight, no pitch.
3. Process post — difference between automation and a system. Before/after architecture comparison.

**Outreach prep:**
- Run Kritano audits on 10 target businesses (mix of legal, accountancy, agency)
- Record 60-90 second Loom per audit showing 2-3 findings
- Draft personalised outreach emails (each references the specific audit)
- Queue for post-launch. Do NOT send yet.

### Week 2: Content readiness, soft signals

**LinkedIn (2 posts):**
4. Sector-specific insight teasing a launch blog post
5. Short take on transparent pricing

**Content QA:**
- Blog posts and case studies finalised and loaded into CMS (draft status)
- All page copy reviewed against brand guidelines
- Apply form tested end-to-end

## Launch Day (May 19, Monday)

**Morning (before 9am):**
1. Deploy site (flip DNS)
2. Publish blog posts and case studies (change to published in CMS)
3. Submit sitemap to GSC
4. Request indexing on priority pages
5. Verify all redirects return 301
6. Run Kritano audit on live site — fix anything flagged

**Mid-morning (10am):**
7. LinkedIn launch post: "chrisgarlick.com is live. Built on a CMS I wrote from scratch." One-liner, link, screenshot.

**Afternoon:**
8. Send first 3 personalised outreach emails with Loom videos
9. Share in relevant communities (where already a member)

## Post-Launch Weeks 1–4

### Week 1 (May 19–23)
- Send remaining 7 outreach emails (10 total in week 1)
- LinkedIn: 2 posts (audit findings + GSC data from new site)
- Monitor GSC coverage, Plausible traffic, Resend dashboard

### Week 2 (May 26–30)
- 5 more outreach emails (new targets, fresh audits)
- Follow up on week 1 non-responses (one follow-up only)
- LinkedIn: 2 posts (case study deep-dive + opinion post)
- Draft blog post 3

### Week 3 (Jun 2–6)
- 5 more outreach emails
- Warm leads get more detailed Loom or call proposal
- LinkedIn: 2 posts (aggregate audit insights + process transparency)
- Publish blog post 3 if ready

### Week 4 (Jun 9–13)
- Final batch of 5 outreach emails (30 total in 4 weeks)
- Pipeline review: categorise Hot / Warm / Cold / No Response
- LinkedIn: 2 posts (results transparency + forward-looking)
- Full metrics review against OKRs

## Channel Strategy

| Channel | Purpose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Primary discovery. Building-in-public + insight posts. | 2-3/week |
| Direct email | Personalised outreach with Kritano audit + Loom. | 5-10/week |
| Blog | AEO-optimised content for organic discovery. | 1-2/month |
| GitHub | Kritano CMS repo as credibility signal. | Ongoing |
| GSC | Monitor indexing and impressions. | Daily → weekly |

**Not used at launch:** Twitter/X, paid ads, email newsletter, YouTube, podcasts.
