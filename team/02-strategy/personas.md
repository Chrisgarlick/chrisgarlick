# Target Personas

---

## Persona 1: "Sarah" — Law Firm Partner

**Role:** Managing Partner, 15-person commercial law firm (Manchester)
**Age:** 44
**Revenue:** £2.5M/year
**Tech maturity:** Low-medium. Uses Clio for case management, Microsoft 365, still prints contracts.

### Goals
- Reduce time lawyers spend on document drafting and client intake
- Improve response time to new enquiries (currently 24-48 hrs)
- Increase fee-earning hours without hiring more associates
- Look modern to younger clients without taking big tech risks

### Frustrations
- Junior associates spend 40% of their time on templated documents
- Client onboarding takes 5-7 touchpoints before work starts
- Heard about AI from the Law Society newsletter but doesn't know what's real vs hype
- Got burned by an IT consultancy who over-promised and under-delivered on a "digital transformation"
- Doesn't want to commit to a £50k project to "explore AI"

### Buying Behaviour
- Trusts recommendations from other partners, professional networks
- Will Google "AI for law firms UK" and read 2-3 articles before reaching out
- Needs to see specific legal use cases — generic "we do AI" means nothing
- Wants fixed pricing — will not accept open-ended day rates
- Needs to feel the person understands law firm operations, not just technology

### What Wins Sarah Over
- A free Kritano audit of her firm's website showing specific gaps
- A case study showing document automation with specific hours saved
- Fixed pricing with clear scope (Build: £5k-8k)
- The fact that Chris maintains the system monthly — she doesn't want to manage software
- Architecture diagrams that show exactly what gets built

### Key Objection
"How do I know this is secure? We handle sensitive client data."

### Content That Reaches Her
- "What AI Implementation Actually Means for a Law Firm" (blog)
- LinkedIn posts shared by other legal professionals
- Direct Loom outreach showing a real finding from her firm's website

## Persona 2: "Tom" — Agency Founder

**Role:** Founder/MD, 8-person digital marketing agency (London)
**Age:** 35
**Revenue:** £800k/year
**Tech maturity:** High. Uses Notion, Slack, Figma, various SaaS. Has tried ChatGPT for content.

### Goals
- Automate repetitive client reporting (currently 2 days/month per client)
- Build AI-powered content production pipeline to increase output without hiring
- Offer "AI services" to his own clients as an upsell
- Reduce operational overhead so he can focus on growth

### Frustrations
- Team is maxed out — hiring is expensive and slow
- Tried building automations with Make/Zapier — they break, nobody maintains them
- Knows AI could help but doesn't have time to figure out what to build
- Worried about competitors who are already marketing "AI-powered" services
- Has been pitched by 3 different "AI automation" freelancers on LinkedIn — all seemed shallow

### Buying Behaviour
- Researches heavily — will check GitHub, LinkedIn, read blog posts, look at the tech stack
- Respects operators who build their own tools (he's technical enough to appreciate it)
- Moves fast once convinced — can make a buying decision in a week
- Values ongoing partnership over one-off projects
- Price-sensitive but understands value — £5k-8k is fine if the ROI is clear

### What Wins Tom Over
- Seeing that Chris built his own CMS and the site runs on it
- Architecture of a content pipeline or reporting automation
- The retainer model — he wants someone who stays and iterates
- Open-source Kritano CMS on GitHub — he can inspect the code
- Real talk about what AI can and can't do (no hype)

### Key Objection
"Can you really deliver at this level as a solo operator?"

### Content That Reaches Him
- "The 3 Workflows Every Agency Should Automate First" (blog)
- GitHub activity showing real code
- LinkedIn "building in public" posts
- A personalised Loom showing what Kritano found on his agency's site

## Persona 3: "David" — Accountancy Practice Director

**Role:** Practice Director, 30-person accountancy firm (Birmingham)
**Age:** 52
**Revenue:** £4M/year
**Tech maturity:** Low. Uses Xero/Sage, Microsoft 365, paper-heavy processes.

### Goals
- Automate client onboarding (currently 3 weeks from first contact to engagement letter)
- Reduce time spent on compliance document preparation
- Free up senior staff from data entry and reconciliation
- Modernise the firm's image for next-generation clients

### Frustrations
- Staff spend hours on manual data entry that should be automated
- Onboarding a new client involves 12+ touchpoints across email, post, and phone
- The firm's IT is managed by a local MSP who doesn't understand AI
- Partners are resistant to change but acknowledge the firm needs to modernise
- Compliance deadlines create seasonal crunch — AI could smooth the workload

### Buying Behaviour
- Slow and methodical — will want a detailed proposal before committing
- Trusts professional networks (ICAEW events, peer recommendations)
- Needs buy-in from other partners — Chris must provide materials David can present internally
- Risk-averse — wants to see proof before committing significant budget
- Prefers a phased approach: start small, expand if it works

### What Wins David Over
- A Kritano audit report he can show partners as "this is where we are"
- A clear, phased proposal: audit first (low commitment), then build
- Case study showing onboarding automation with specific time savings
- Monthly reporting on what the system handled — partners need visibility
- Fixed pricing — no surprises

### Key Objection
"Our data is sensitive and regulated. How does this comply with GDPR and professional standards?"

### Content That Reaches Him
- Sector-specific blog posts (accounting-focused)
- Recommendations from peers at professional events
- A well-formatted PDF audit report attached to a warm introduction email
