# Strategy — Final Output

## Executive Summary

Chris Garlick is positioning as a **software developer** — not a consultant, not an agency, not an "AI specialist". He builds proprietary tools (Kritano for site intelligence, Kritano CMS for content infrastructure), uses AI where it makes sense in his delivery workflow, and offers fixed-fee engagements to professional service firms and agencies.

The competitive landscape is crowded with generic "AI automation" operators, but Chris's differentiation is concrete: he builds his own tools, the site runs on his own CMS, every case study shows architecture not just outcomes, and pricing is transparent. The moat is shallow at pre-revenue but compounds with every engagement — proprietary tooling, sector playbooks, and process proof that can't be faked.

Target sectors are legal/accountancy (Tier 1) and agencies (Tier 1), with local service businesses and e-commerce as Tier 2 expansion. Three personas guide all content and outreach: Sarah (law firm partner, risk-averse, needs specific legal use cases), Tom (agency founder, technically curious, moves fast), and David (accountancy director, methodical, needs internal buy-in materials).

Launch OKRs are focused: site live, 2 case studies + 2 blog posts published, 20 Kritano audits run against target businesses, 10 personalised outreach messages sent, 1 paid engagement closed within 90 days.

## Key Decisions & Recommendations

- Position as "Software Developer" — never "AI expert", "AI specialist", "consultant", or "agency"
- One-liner: "I build software that replaces manual work. Some of it uses AI. All of it ships."
- Tagline: "Audit. Build. Maintain."
- The /team skill stays private — frame delivery advantage as "AI-integrated workflow" not a product
- Process proof over social proof — architecture diagrams, quantified outcomes, live systems
- Fixed pricing shown on site: Build £5k-8k, Retainer £3k-6k/mo
- Free Kritano audit is the primary outreach weapon
- "Built on Kritano CMS" in the footer — the site is the proof
- No stock photography. No testimonials. No fake social proof.
- Sector focus: legal/accountancy + agencies (Tier 1). Expand to Tier 2 after first wins.

## Dependencies & Handoffs

**For Marketing:** Use the positioning statement, one-liner, and tagline options as the foundation for brand guidelines. The personas define the voice — sharp, engineering-minded, zero fluff. Not friendly-corporate. Not consultant-speak. Closer to a developer's calling card.

**For Design:** The "editorial operator" aesthetic from the spec aligns with the positioning — dark, precise, typographic. The design should feel like it was made by someone with taste who also ships code.

**For Content:** Blog posts must target specific persona queries (see "Content That Reaches Her/Him" in each persona). AEO-optimised. Every post links to `/apply` and a case study.

**For Software:** The site running on Kritano CMS is itself a positioning proof point. "Built on Kritano CMS" must be visible. Case studies must follow Problem → Architecture → Outcome format.

## Open Questions & Risks

1. **Pricing validation** — £5k-8k for a Build and £3k-6k/mo for a Retainer are assumptions. Will be validated through prospect conversations in the first 90 days.
2. **Sector response** — We believe legal/accountancy and agencies are the right Tier 1 sectors but have no data yet. OKRs include tracking response rates by sector.
3. **Solo operator objection** — "Can you really deliver at this level alone?" is the most likely objection. The answer (purpose-built tooling and smart use of AI) needs to be convincing on the site, not just stated.
