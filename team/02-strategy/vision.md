# Vision & Mission

## Vision
Every service business runs software that was built for how they actually work — not adapted from generic templates, not bolted on by consultancies, not abandoned after a strategy deck.

## Mission
Build and maintain software systems for professional service firms using proprietary tools, transparent architecture, and ongoing operational partnership. One developer. No fluff. Systems that get used.

## Values

### 1. Build the tools, then build with them
Don't resell. Don't rebrand. Build the infrastructure yourself, then use it to deliver. The tools are the proof and the advantage.

### 2. Process proof over social proof
Show how it works, not who said it works. Architecture diagrams, quantified outcomes, live systems. Let the work speak when there are no testimonials to lean on.

### 3. Outcomes over activity
A 40-page strategy deck is activity. A system that saves 15 hours a week is an outcome. Only the second one matters.

### 4. Fixed scope, honest pricing
No open-ended day rates. No scope creep dressed up as "discovery." Tell the client what it costs, what they get, and when it's done.

### 5. Stay small on purpose
Solo operation with purpose-built tooling is a feature, not a limitation. No juniors learning on client projects. No layers of account management. One developer, fully accountable, moving fast.
