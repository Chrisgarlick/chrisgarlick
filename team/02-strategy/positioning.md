# Positioning Statement

## Primary Positioning

For **professional service firms and agency operators** who know their operations need automating but don't know where to start or who to trust, **Chris Garlick** is a **software developer** who audits your business, builds working systems that replace manual work, and maintains them month-to-month — all using proprietary tools he built himself. Unlike consultancies who deliver slide decks, agencies who resell off-the-shelf automations, or freelancers who disappear after handoff, Chris builds the tools, runs the tools, and ships measurable outcomes. Some of those systems use AI. All of them ship.

## Supporting Statements

**For prospects who ask "why not hire a developer?"**
Because a typical developer builds what you brief. Chris audits what you actually need, builds the system, and maintains it. You're not buying code — you're buying an ongoing operational advantage.

**For prospects who ask "why not use an agency?"**
Because agencies layer margin on top of off-the-shelf tools. Chris built the audit platform (Kritano) and the delivery infrastructure himself. There's no middleman, no resold SaaS, and no junior dev learning on your project.

**For prospects who ask "why solo?"**
Because purpose-built tooling and smart use of AI means one developer with the right infrastructure delivers faster, cheaper, and with more consistency than a traditional team. The site you're reading runs on his CMS. That's the proof.
