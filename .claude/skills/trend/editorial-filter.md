# Editorial Filter — What's Worth Covering

Not every trending topic deserves a post. This filter ensures we only create content that serves the audience, builds authority, and aligns with Chris's positioning as an AI integration expert.

## The Three Gates

Every trend must pass **all three gates** before becoming content:

### Gate 1: Relevance

**Does this directly relate to at least one of the 6 pillars?**

- Yes: Anthropic launches new MCP features for Claude > AI Agents & Automation
- Yes: Study shows AI coding tools increase developer productivity by 40% > AI-Assisted Development
- Yes: New EU AI Act compliance deadline announced > AI Ethics & Governance
- No: General tech industry layoffs > Not relevant
- No: Consumer AI product launch (AI photo editor, etc.) > Not relevant unless it has dev/integration implications
- No: Pure ML research with no practical application yet > Not relevant

**Does it affect developers, CTOs, or businesses considering AI integration?**

If the answer is "only indirectly" or "only if you squint", skip it.

### Gate 2: Substance

**Is there enough here to say something useful?**

- Yes: Official announcement with details > Strong substance
- Yes: Research study with data > Strong substance
- Yes: Developing story with confirmed facts > Moderate substance
- Maybe: Rumour or leak without confirmation > Wait for confirmation
- No: Vague speculation > Skip
- No: Repackaged old news > Skip
- No: "X might happen soon" without evidence > Skip

**Can we add insight beyond just reporting?**

Chris's content should always include a practitioner's angle - how this actually affects the work of integrating AI into teams and workflows. If all we can do is restate the headline, it's not worth a post.

### Gate 3: Engagement Potential

**Will the audience care?**

Score each trend on these signals:

| Signal | Weight | Examples |
|--------|--------|---------|
| **Affects many developers/businesses** | High | New LLM API, major tool update |
| **Has a strong data point** | High | "Teams using AI tools ship 40% faster" |
| **Controversial or debated** | High | AI replacing developers, code ownership |
| **Actionable** | High | "Do this now to improve your AI workflow" |
| **Surprising** | Medium | Counter-intuitive findings about AI productivity |
| **Breaking / very recent** | Medium | First 48 hours of a story |
| **Niche but deep** | Low | Specific API edge case |
| **Already saturated** | Negative | If every AI influencer already covered it identically |

**Minimum threshold**: At least 2 "High" signals, or 1 "High" + 2 "Medium".

## Angle Selection

Once a trend passes all three gates, choose the angle:

### Available Angles

| Angle | Tone | Best when... |
|-------|------|-------------|
| **The Explainer** | Educational, clear | Complex topic that needs demystifying for non-AI-native teams |
| **The Hot Take** | Opinionated, confident | We have a strong, defensible opinion from hands-on experience |
| **The Data Drop** | Analytical, authoritative | There's a compelling stat to lead with |
| **The Warning** | Urgent, helpful | Something changed and teams need to adapt |
| **The Opportunity** | Optimistic, strategic | A change that early adopters can benefit from |
| **The Myth Buster** | Corrective, honest | Common misunderstanding about AI integration |
| **The Practitioner** | Hands-on, practical | We can share real workflow changes or tool configurations |

### Angle Rules

- **Never be alarmist** - even "warning" content should be calm and solution-oriented
- **Always include what to do** - don't just report the problem
- **Credit original sources** - never present others' research as our own
- **Be honest about uncertainty** - if something is developing, say so
- **Avoid bandwagoning** - if every other AI account is posting the same take, find a different angle or skip it
- **Practitioner credibility** - wherever possible, tie the angle back to real experience integrating AI into workflows

## Content Quality Checks

Before finalising any content piece, verify:

- [ ] **Factually accurate** - claims are sourced and verified
- [ ] **Source attributed** - data, quotes, and findings credited to original source
- [ ] **Adds value** - not just restating the headline
- [ ] **Chris's voice** - conversational expert, not corporate robot
- [ ] **British English** - optimise, colour, favour, etc.
- [ ] **Actionable** - reader should know what to do after reading
- [ ] **Timely** - will this still be relevant when it's posted?
- [ ] **Platform-appropriate** - tone and format match the platform
- [ ] **Practitioner angle** - includes insight from someone who actually builds with these tools

---

## Kill Criteria

**Immediately discard a trend if:**

1. It's **only** relevant to one specific niche framework nobody uses
2. It's a **PR piece** disguised as news (company announcing their own product as "industry news")
3. The source is **unreliable** and no other sources confirm it
4. It's **more than 14 days old** with no new developments
5. Covering it would require **speculation** about unconfirmed details
6. The only angle is **fear** ("AI will replace all developers!") without balanced context
7. It's **pure hype** with no practical implications for people actually building with AI
