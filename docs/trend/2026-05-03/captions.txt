48% of companies say AI adoption has been a "massive disappointment." But the tools aren't the problem.

93% of developers use AI. 97% of execs have deployed AI agents. Adoption is nearly universal.

So why are results so poor?

Because most companies bought tools and called it transformation. No workflow redesign. No proper training. No measurement beyond "how many licences did we activate?"

The data is wild - AI actually slowed experienced developers down by 19% in a controlled study. But they thought they were 20% faster.

Meanwhile, AI super-users save 9 hours a week. Same tools. Completely different results.

The difference isn't the technology. It's the implementation.

If your AI strategy starts with "which tool should we buy?" you're asking the wrong question. Start with "which workflows should we redesign?"
